# HER Net logo concepts, round 1

`HER-Net-Logo-Concepts-Round-1.pdf`: 7-page presentation of three minimalist concepts, a corporate treatment of the current HER Net logo, in blue (#0A5FBF), green (#059669), dark gray (#2B2F36) and white.

| Concept | Idea | Type |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Her Signal | A woman whose long hair flows as tapering signal waves; green outer wave for sustainability | Outfit SemiBold |
| 2 · Venus Growth | The women (Venus) symbol as a network node with a signal inside; the cross becomes a seedling | Manrope Bold |
| 3 · Rising Figure | A woman whose body is a leaf, under two signal arcs | Plus Jakarta Sans Bold |

`svg/<concept>/`: for each concept, `icon`, `lockup-horizontal` and `lockup-stacked`, each in `color`, `reverse` (for dark gray), `mono` (dark gray) and `mono-white`. Type is converted to outlines; backgrounds are transparent.

These are concepts for review, not final artwork. After a direction is chosen: refine, proof colors, check trademarks, and produce the final logo kit.

## Round 2 · AN meets HER (`round-2/`)

`round-2/HER-Net-Logo-Concepts-Round-2.pdf`: 8 pages. Corporate, minimalist lettermarks playing with AN (Access Network) and HER (High-Efficiency Resilient).

| Concept | Idea |
|---|---|
| 4 · A for Her | The A of AN is a woman (floating head, geometric A); the N's diagonal runs parallel to her leg |
| 5 · H Meets N | N (Access Network) and H (HER) share two stems; the blue diagonal weaves over the green crossbar |
| 6 · Three Bars of HER | The E of HER is three bars, one per word: High · Efficiency · Resilient; custom H≡R wordmark |
| Recommended | H Meets N symbol with the H≡R wordmark (`svg/7-recommended-hybrid/`) |

Each concept folder has the symbol and horizontal lockup in color, reverse, mono and mono-white, plus `construction.svg` on a 120-unit grid.
