# Access Network website mockup

Five-page static mockup of a website for **Access Network, powered by RA Omega Cable TV Corporation**, written for a European audience: EU institutions, Team Europe agencies and European companies. All content comes from the *Access Network Company Profile 2026*.

| Page | File | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Home | `index.html` | Positioning, key facts, service-area map, 2023–2028 roadmap, EU alignment, ICT Forum |
| About | `about.html` | Story and milestones, mission and vision, values, collaboration model, group companies |
| Solutions | `solutions.html` | Who we serve and the five service lines |
| Smart Agriculture | `smart-agriculture.html` | Focus areas, research ecosystem, field-to-network concept, training, ICT Forum series |
| Partner with us | `partner.html` | Alignment with the EU–Philippines Digital Economy Package, partnership models, track record, contact form |

## View it

Open `index.html` in a browser. There is no build step or dependency; fonts load from Google Fonts. Shared styles and scripts are in `assets/`.

## Mockup notes

- The enquiry form is not connected. Submitting shows a notice and sends nothing.
- The map on the home page is schematic: town positions come from coordinates, and the shaded area shows the service footprint, not cable routes.
- References to the EU–Philippines Digital Economy Package and Global Gateway describe alignment with published priorities, not an agreement. No EU emblem is used.
- No photography is included. Real photos of field crews, training sessions and the ICT Forum should replace the graphic panels before launch.
