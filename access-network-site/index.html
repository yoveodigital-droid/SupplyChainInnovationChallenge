Who we are

We don’t just lay fibre

Access Network started as a trusted local cable TV and internet provider for the rural municipalities of Nueva Ecija. We saw that the heart of the Philippines beats in its rural communities, and that these were often the last places to receive the tools of the modern world.

In 2023 we began our move to a full-scale ICT social enterprise, with broadband and fibre optics at the core. We measure success on a double bottom line: technical performance and social impact.

As a women-led organisation, we believe digitalisation should not be a privilege of the city. It is a right for every community.

Where we work

The rice granary of the Philippines

Nueva Ecija is the country’s largest rice-producing province, two to three hours north of Metro Manila. Our networks serve its upland and lowland towns.

Strategic plan 2023–2028

Four pillars, one roadmap

Innovate

Adapt technology to rural terrain, integrating IoT, ICT and solar power.

Connect

Connect people to opportunity: remote work, e-learning and digital commerce.

Collaborate

Partner with local government, CSOs, community leaders and ICT stakeholders for measurable social impact.

Grow

Scale our reach and services, evolving from service provider to long-term development partner.

For European partners

A local partner for Europe’s digital agenda in the Philippines

Our roadmap lines up with the published priorities of the EU–Philippines Digital Economy Package under Global Gateway: connectivity, cybersecurity skills, data for better decisions and research collaboration.
