From cable TV
to rural ICT

Access Network is a women-led social enterprise redefining the rural digital landscape. We began as a local cable TV and internet provider and are now becoming a full-scale information and communications technology company.

Our story

Fifteen years in the same communities

In 2011 Access Network began with a simple, essential mission: bring cable TV and internet to the rural municipalities of Nueva Ecija.

As the world moved faster, we knew we had to do more than keep up. In 2023 we took the step from traditional service provider to comprehensive ICT social enterprise, with broadband and fibre optics as our core.

We aren’t just laying cables. We are building the infrastructure for dreams to grow.

Purpose

Mission & vision

Vision

To be recognised as the leading company that promotes rural social development by making information technology and communications truly accessible and transformative.

Mission

To provide world-class ICT products and services tailored for rural communities, and to promote and integrate ICT solutions that serve as the backbone for modern rural development.

Women-led

Technology as a catalyst for empathy and equity

We are not just building a network. We are building a legacy where our rural farmers, students and entrepreneurs have the world at their fingertips.

Our women-led management team puts sustainable, community-first growth ahead of short-term scale. As a social enterprise, our double bottom line means we report on social impact alongside network performance.
