Alignment

Where our work meets the EU–Philippines Digital Economy Package

The package, part of the EU’s Global Gateway strategy, focuses on connectivity, earth-observation data, 5G and cybersecurity skills, and research collaboration. Here is what we can contribute on the ground.

PriorityWhat Access Network bringsPossible joint activitySDGs
Digital connectivity Broadband and fibre networks across rural Nueva Ecija since 2011; in-house design, laying and splicing. Last-mile extensions to schools, health units and farm sites; rural deployment pilots. SDG 9SDG 4
Cybersecurity & digital skills Fibre optic and digital jobs training; cybersecurity awareness; annual ICT Forum since 2024. Co-designed curricula and train-the-trainer programmes with European experts. SDG 4SDG 8
Earth observation & data Field connectivity and IoT sensing for agriculture, close to PhilRice and CLSU. Pilots that combine Copernicus data with in-field sensor data for farmers and LGUs. SDG 2SDG 13
Research & innovation Working relationships with CLSU and NEUST; a test bed in a real rural network. EU–Philippine university projects that need a connectivity and field partner. SDG 9SDG 17
Inclusion & green transition Women-led management; solar installation and integration; double-bottom-line reporting. Gender-responsive digital inclusion activities; solar-powered network sites. SDG 5SDG 7

References to the EU–Philippines Digital Economy Package describe how our work aligns with its published priorities. They do not imply an existing agreement or endorsement.
