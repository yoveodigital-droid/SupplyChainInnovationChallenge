# 12. Content audit {#content-audit}

**Sample figures to verify or replace.** None of these appear in the Access Network profile:

- Hero: 100% women-led teams, 45+ rural pilots connected, 3,200+ farmers empowered
- Node card: Hub #12, 120 Mbps, 99.8% uptime, 14 live nodes, 18 technicians
- Impact: 18 municipalities, 450+ women tech trainees, 35 smart agri deployments, 240 km fiber
- Map: 22 / 15 / 18 nodes and 12 / 18 / 15 technicians per hub
- Testimonial: 35% less water waste, team of 5 women
- Response promise: "within 24–48 hours"

**Sample people and places.** Maria Corazon, Jenny Santos and Arlene Gutierrez are invented names with invented quotes. Benguet, Isabela and Bukidnon are not Access Network service areas. The address "HER Net Innovation Hub, Luzon" and the email partner@hernet.org are placeholders.

**Internal inconsistencies**

- Hero says 45+ rural pilots, but the Impact section says 35 smart agri deployments and 18 municipalities.
- The hero card says Hub #12 has 18 technicians assigned, while the map says Benguet has 12.
- The map's technician totals (12 + 18 + 15 = 45) don't relate to the 450+ trainees figure.

**Broken or empty links**

- "About Us" (menu) and "Mission & Vision" (footer) point to `#about`, but the page has no About section.
- Focus area links, social icons, Privacy Policy, Terms of Partnership and ESG Reporting have no destination.
- The partnership form shows a success message but sends nothing.

**Wording to review**

- "Fiber OSP" is industry jargon (outside plant); spell it out for general audiences.
- "Telemetric node" and "Soil N-P-K Bal." are technical; fine for the demo, but consider plainer labels.
- Mixed capitalization of "Smart Agri" / "smart agri" across the page.
