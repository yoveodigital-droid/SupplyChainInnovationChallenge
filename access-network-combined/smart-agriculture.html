Focus feature

Plant the seeds
of a smarter harvest

Our most ambitious goal is to make Nueva Ecija a leader in smart agriculture. With farmers, local government and technology partners, we are bringing ICT into the fields of the country’s rice granary.

Why here

World-class research in our own backyard

Few rural areas have national agricultural research agencies and two state universities within a short drive. Nueva Ecija does, which gives it the potential to lead the Philippines in agricultural technology. Our networks can link that research to the farms around it.

  • PhilRicePhilippine Rice Research Institute
  • PhilMechPhilippine Center for Postharvest Development and Mechanization
  • PCCPhilippine Carabao Center
  • CLSU · NEUSTCentral Luzon State University · Nueva Ecija University of Science and Technology
