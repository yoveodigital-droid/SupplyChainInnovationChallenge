Questions

Before you ask

Which areas do you cover?

Our core network serves San Jose City, Rizal, Llanera, Carranglan and Pantabangan. Fiber, IT, CCTV and solar projects elsewhere in Nueva Ecija are scoped site by site.

Can one team handle internet, IT and cameras?

Yes. We handle connectivity, system integration, managed support, CCTV and solar, so one team is accountable for the whole setup.

Do you work with LGUs, schools and other institutions?

Yes. Our enterprise services are built for businesses, institutions and government, and DICT Region 3 named us an Outstanding Partner in 2024 and 2025.

Can we buy materials only?

Yes. We supply fiber and networking hardware for your own teams or contractors.

Can you train our staff?

Yes. We run fiber optic training, digital jobs training and cybersecurity awareness sessions.
