HER Net: community-centered connectivity for Nueva Ecija

HER Net is the network and program Access Network is building across Nueva Ecija’s 2nd District. It combines resilient infrastructure, field data, digital platforms and ICT training, and it is led by women from the towns it serves.

Explore live dashboard → Program areas

What HER Net is

A network by Access Network, built with the community

Access Network is the company: a women-led ICT social enterprise serving Nueva Ecija since 2011. HER Net is the network and program it builds and runs in the 2nd District.

The name stands for High-Efficiency Resilient Network. “HER” also points to the women-led team behind it, from management to the technicians who splice the fiber.

Foundational ecosystem

Four Pillars of Inclusive Digital Transformation

Combining gender empowerment, last-mile connectivity, climate resilience, and agricultural technology to build sustainable communities.

Message pillar A

Women in ICT & Tech

Technology is a catalyst for empathy and equity. We train local women and youth to build, run and own the network.

  • Fiber optic training: vocational programs that build local technical talent.
  • Women’s bootcamps: fiber optics and tower safety for young women.
  • Digital jobs training: skills for online and remote work.
  • Women-led management: sustainable, community-first growth.
Message pillar C

Smart Ag & Rural Broadband

Connectivity in the fields, so farmers know their soil, their weather and their price.

  • Precision smart agri: real-time soil, weather and pest monitoring.
  • Direct market access: cooperatives linked to urban buyers and digital trading.
  • Climate early warning: typhoon, flood and drought alerts.
  • Smart agri extension centers: digital farmer registration at municipal agriculture offices.
Message pillar D · Infrastructure

Resilient and Sustainable Infrastructure

Built for typhoons, landslides and brownouts, and powered by the sun where the grid can’t reach.

  • Fiber backbone: across six LGUs, with last-mile extensions.
  • Solar-powered nodes: for off-grid and unreliable sites.
  • Satellite backhaul: low-Earth orbit links with micro-solar for mountain-pass barangays.
  • Barangay digital hubs: solar-powered public Wi-Fi for e-governance and emergencies.
Message pillars B & D · User

Digital Safety & Literacy

A connection is only as useful as the confidence to use it safely.

  • Cybersecurity awareness: safety and protection for rural communities online.
  • IoT awareness: making IoT understandable for farmers and cooperatives.
  • ICT Forum: an annual forum since 2024, on cybersecurity and smart agri.
  • School connectivity: fiber to last-mile public schools in Llanera, Rizal and Carranglan.

Building the local digital ecosystem

Four layers, built locally

Each layer depends on the one below it. Together they keep value, skills and data in the district.

1

Infrastructure

Fiber backbone and last-mile fiber, solar-powered nodes, satellite backhaul for GIDA passes, barangay digital hubs, colocation and disaster recovery sites.

2

Data

Soil, weather and pest sensors; climate early warning; digital farmer registration; links to PhilRice, PhilMech, PCC and CLSU research.

3

Platform

Direct market access for cooperatives, e-governance, telehealth, e-learning, MDRRMO disaster command and the live dashboard.

4

User

Farmers, cooperatives, MSMEs, students, health workers and LGU staff, trained in ICT, digital safety and literacy.
