Nueva Ecija’s 2nd District

The northern half of the country’s rice granary: a third of the province’s land, its national agri research hub, its irrigation source at Pantabangan Dam, and most of its geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas (GIDA).

Sources: PSA 2020/2024, DOH GIDA Registry, DICT Region III, DENR, via the 2026 stakeholder baseline. “~” marks estimates.

Geographic isolation

Two districts in one

Most of the district’s land holds a small share of its people, and that is exactly where the network stops.

Lowland · urban core

San Jose, Muñoz, Llanera, Lupao

  • Land~42% (~799 km²)
  • People~85% of the district
  • TerrainFlat alluvial farm plains
  • Density370–810 people / km²

Economic drivers

Livelihoods and national agency hubs

  • Agri-tech & seedScience City of Muñoz drives national rice research and seed multiplication.
  • Hydro & fisheriesPantabangan Dam supplies power, irrigation through NIA-UPRIIS, and inland tilapia.
  • TradeSan Jose City is the commercial gateway to Cagayan Valley (Region 2).
  • Upland cropsCorn, onion and vegetables in Carranglan, Lupao and Rizal.
  • People64.5% of working age; 28.6% under 15, higher in upland LGUs. 51.2% male, 48.8% female.
