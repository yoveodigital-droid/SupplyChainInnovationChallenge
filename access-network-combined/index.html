Who we are

We don’t just lay fiber

Access Network started as a trusted local cable TV and internet provider for the rural municipalities of Nueva Ecija. We saw that the heart of the Philippines beats in its rural communities, and that these were often the last places to receive the tools of the modern world.

In 2023 we began our move to a full-scale ICT social enterprise, with broadband and fiber optics at the core. We measure success on a double bottom line: technical performance and social impact.

As a women-led organization, we believe digitalization should not be a privilege of the city. It is a right for every community.

Where we work

The rice granary of the Philippines

Nueva Ecija is the country’s largest rice-producing province, two to three hours north of Metro Manila. Our networks serve its upland and lowland towns.

  • Our communitiesRizal · Llanera · Carranglan · Pantabangan · San Jose City
  • Research on our doorstepPhilRice · PhilMech · Philippine Carabao Center · CLSU · NEUST
  • Our ambitionMake Nueva Ecija the ICT center of Central Luzon, leading the nation in smart farming
We don’t just see cables and servers. We see the children who can finally attend online classes, the farmers who can check market prices, and the local businesses that can grow beyond their borders.
