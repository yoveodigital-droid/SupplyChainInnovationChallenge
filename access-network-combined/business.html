How a project runs

From site survey to support

  1. Site survey

    Our engineers visit your site and map routes, power and equipment rooms.

  2. Design & proposal

    You receive a proposed design, bill of materials and scope of work.

  3. Build & install

    Our crews lay and splice fiber, install equipment and commission the system.

  4. Handover & training

    We document the setup and train your staff to use and look after it.

  5. Support

    A local team stays on call for maintenance and changes.
