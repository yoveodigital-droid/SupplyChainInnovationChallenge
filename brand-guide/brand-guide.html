01 · Brand foundation

Who we are

Access Network is a women-led ICT social enterprise in Nueva Ecija, Philippines. We began in 2011 as a local cable TV and internet provider and are becoming a full-scale information and communications technology company.

We believe better connectivity builds stronger communities. We combine high-speed infrastructure with digital solutions so that our rural home can lead the country in smart agriculture. We don’t just lay fiber; we plant the seeds for a sustainable, tech-driven future.

Vision

To be recognized as the leading company that promotes rural social development by making information technology and communications truly accessible and transformative.

Mission

To provide world-class ICT products and services tailored for rural communities, and to promote and integrate ICT solutions that serve as the backbone for modern rural development.

Reserved for the word “that” in the tagline lockup, as in the company profile. Never for headlines, body text or other words.

Hierarchy recipe

Eyebrow label with a short blue rule → condensed uppercase headline → lead paragraph in Slate → body. Use it to open every section.

One accent word

Color one key word of a headline in Signal Blue, e.g. “Business connectivity”. Never the whole headline, never two words apart.

Line length

Keep body text to 60–75 characters per line. Align left; don’t justify or center long text.

Numbers

Use tabular figures in tables and fact rows so digits line up.

05 · Graphic elements

Strands, rules & labels

Fiber strands

Curved bands from the company profile cover: four to five strands in Signal Blue, Ink and a soft tint, sweeping top to bottom.

Using the strands

  • Bleed them off the right or top edge; never show both ends.
  • One strand group per page or screen, behind the content, never through text.
  • On dark grounds, swap Ink for Frost and the tint for Night Raised.
  • Online, a thin light pulse may travel along the strands; switch it off for reduced-motion settings.

Eyebrow rule

Where we work

A 2 px blue rule plus a mono label opens each section.

Data labels

15.81°N 121.14°E

Real coordinates, years and agency names in mono. Facts as texture, never fake numbers.

Pillar dots

Innovate Connect

Blue dots mark the four roadmap pillars only.

Schematic maps

Towns as open blue circles, partners as ink squares, service area as a soft blue shape. Label each with a caption saying it is schematic.

05 · Graphic elements

Photography & icons

Show

  • Our own crews splicing fiber, on poles and in the field
  • Women leaders and technicians at work
  • Farmers, students and shop owners using the connection
  • Training sessions and the ICT Forum
  • Real places: rice fields, the uplands, town centers

Avoid

  • Generic stock of glowing globes and server corridors
  • Staged photos of people who aren’t our customers
  • Heavy filters, tilted horizons, cut-out backgrounds
  • Images of children without written parental consent
  • Partner logos or uniforms used without permission

Treat

  • Natural daylight, true color, slight warmth
  • Leave calm space for a headline or logo
  • Caption with place and date: “Rizal, Nueva Ecija · May 2026”
  • Credit the photographer
  • Get consent forms from everyone identifiable

Icon style

Simple outline icons on a 24 px grid, 1.75 px stroke, rounded ends, in Ink. Only a single accent icon per group in Signal Blue. Use a consistent open-source outline set, such as Lucide, and don’t mix sets.

06 · Voice & messaging

Voice & tone

Plain

Short sentences, everyday words. “Internet for your shop”, not “last-mile broadband enablement”.

Specific

Name the town, the year, the agency. Facts persuade; adjectives don’t.

Honest

Never promise speeds, coverage or partnerships we can’t show. Say “aligned with”, not “funded by”, unless it’s signed.

Warm

We are neighbors. Write “we” and “you”. Talk about people before cables.

07 · Digital

Web components

Buttons
Request a quote →HoverSecondary

Condensed uppercase label, 4 px radius. One primary button per view. Arrow only on buttons that move forward.

Form fields

Mono uppercase labels above fields. Focus state: 2 px Signal Blue outline. Error text explains the fix.

Chips & status

SDG 9Core service areaPlaceholder

Blue chips for tags, green for available or agriculture, dashed for content still to come.

Layout grid

Content column up to 1200 px with 16–40 px side gutters. Splits run 5:7 or 4:8. Sections stack to one column below 860 px.

Spacing & shape
4 · 8 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 px

A 4 px base unit. Corners 4 px on buttons, fields and panels; 2 px on chips. Separate content with rules and whitespace before reaching for cards and shadows.

Accessibility

Visible focus on every control, alt text on images, labels on every field, and support for dark mode and reduced motion.

08 · Applications

Co-branding with partners

Access Network
Partner logo
Partner logo

Equal partners

Logos in a row, the same optical height, separated by a thin Line-color divider and clear space X. Our logo comes first on our own materials.

Partner-led materials

Follow the lead partner’s guidelines and provide our full-color or one-color logo files. Don’t restyle it to match.

Government

Use DICT, DOST and LGU seals only with written permission and only on activities they officially support.

EU-funded actions

The EU emblem and funding statement follow the EU’s communication and visibility requirements, applied only when an action is formally funded. Never use the emblem to imply endorsement.

Before you publish
  • Correct logo version from the master files, at or above minimum size
  • Clear space respected; no effects or recoloring
  • Colors from this palette; contrast checked
  • Barlow Condensed, Public Sans and JetBrains Mono only
  • Facts verified: dates, names, agencies, figures
  • No prices, speeds or coverage claims without approval
  • Consent on file for every identifiable person in photos
  • Partner logos approved in writing
  • Contact details and call to action included
  • Spelling convention consistent throughout
